Use case: ui-mockup

Primary request: Create a second original visual direction for a Chinese AI drink-life journal based on a four-module PRD. The direction is called “雨木拼贴 / Rainwood Collage”: more literary, novel and art-directed than a normal journal app, using controlled collage, hand-torn paper poster edges, wet café windows and dark walnut wood. It must still look like a practical, shippable mobile product interface—not concept art, not a marketing website, and not a scrapbook explosion.

Scene/backdrop: one wide 16:9 landscape design-review board on a deep rainy café atmosphere. A nearly black green-charcoal background (`#171916`) with subtle walnut grain (`#2A211C`), soft wet-window blue-green reflections (`#536A6C`), and four complete modern smartphone frames side by side. No hardware logo.

Style/medium: polished Figma-like mobile UI mockup; sophisticated Chinese editorial collage. Materials feel tactile and believable: dark walnut, oat recycled paper (`#E5D7C2`), charcoal ink (`#292420`), translucent tracing paper, one restrained hand-torn edge per screen, one tiny archival paper fragment, rare oxidized brass accent (`#A67C52`), rare wet rose accent (`#8E5C58`). Realistic cinematic rainy-day café drink photography with shallow depth of field, window droplets, warm tungsten reflections and deep coffee browns. Use open-font-like elegant Chinese serif display type with clean Chinese sans-serif UI labels and small archival monospace numbers. Fine rules, small stamped dates, asymmetrical magazine grids, high legibility. Functional text always sits on clean rectangular paper interiors. Controls meet generous touch sizing.

Composition/framing: four different phone screens equally sized and fully visible, aligned on a review board. Each phone uses dark walnut navigation chrome and readable oat-paper content surfaces. Collage fragments may overlap only 8–16px and may never cover the drink, controls, status, prices or important text. Each screen has exactly one hero photo, one torn-paper boundary, one archival fragment, and one accent mark. Use low broad shadow only under real paper scraps; normal controls have no shadows. Bottom navigation consistent across all phones with exactly four labels and delicate line icons: “记录  创作  发布  我的”; correct current tab highlighted in aged brass on each phone.
