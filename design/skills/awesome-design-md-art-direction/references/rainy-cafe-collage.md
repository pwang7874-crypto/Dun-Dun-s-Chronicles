# Rainy Café Collage Recipe

Use this reference only when the requested mood is a deep, literary café, rainy-window, wood, handmade paper, collage, or torn-poster direction.

## Selected upstream documents

The local collection was reviewed at `design/reference/awesome-design-md`.

1. `design-md/starbucks/DESIGN.md` — dominant material role.
   - Confirmed source rules: warm café neutrals, several mapped green depths, deep green feature bands, photographed physical-card feeling, restrained layered elevation, color-block rhythm.
   - Adaptation: replace Starbucks greens and retail/rewards language with walnut, rainy blue-green, oxidized brass, and private-journal semantics. Do not use its logo, proprietary SoDoSans, universal pill buttons, gift-card art, order CTA, or store layout.
2. `design-md/runwayml/DESIGN.md` — lighting and image role.
   - Confirmed source rules: cinematic full-bleed imagery, dark surfaces, zero shadows, cool slate metadata, small radii, asymmetric editorial grids, imagery carrying the atmosphere.
   - Adaptation: use rainy café photography, wet-window reflections, shallow depth of field, and dark wood instead of film-industry imagery. Keep product controls more visible than Runway's marketing UI.
3. `design-md/clay/DESIGN.md` — composition role.
   - Confirmed source rules: art-directed layout, organic handmade artifacts, large feature cards, cream paper surfaces, 4px spacing base, 44px controls, limited accent palette.
   - Adaptation: translate organic 3D shapes into torn paper, translucent tracing paper, tape shadows, stamped numbers, and irregular photo crops. Do not use Clay mascots, proprietary Plain Black, saturated rainbow cards, or its exact rounded component system.

The upstream repository is MIT. The analysis documents describe public design patterns but do not transfer ownership of the source brands' visual identities.

## Resulting original direction

Name: **雨木拼贴 / Rainwood Collage**.

Emotional sentence: a private drink journal assembled beside a rain-streaked café window on a walnut table.

### Dominant material

- Dark walnut canvas with visible but subtle wood grain.
- Warm recycled-paper cards and charcoal ink.
- Wet blue-green glass reflections for inactive or waiting states.
- Oxidized brass as the scarce highlight.

### Semantic palette

- `rainwood/canvas` `#171916` — deepest background, not pure black.
- `walnut/surface` `#2A211C` — navigation, photo surround, deep panels.
- `coffee/raised` `#3A2C25` — selected controls and member card.
- `paper/oat` `#E5D7C2` — readable paper cards and forms.
- `paper/ink` `#292420` — text on paper.
- `rain/glass` `#536A6C` — waiting, analysis, secondary highlights.
- `moss/deep` `#31453E` — privacy, saved, calm success.
- `brass/aged` `#A67C52` — primary action, stamp, focus ring.
- `rose/wet` `#8E5C58` — personal/emotional accent, under 5% of a screen.
- `mist/text` `#C8C3BA` — secondary text on dark surfaces.
- `danger/madder` `#A8554B` — failure/destructive state.

### Typography

- Chinese editorial title: Source Han Serif SC / Noto Serif CJK SC, regular or medium; never fake-calligraphy for core UI.
- Body and controls: Source Han Sans SC / Noto Sans CJK SC or system sans.
- Archival numbers: an open-licensed monospaced font.
- Handwriting: optional single short phrase only; never used for controls or paragraphs.

### Collage grammar

Each screen may use:

- one dominant truthful drink photograph;
- one controlled torn-paper edge around a content layer;
- one small archival fragment such as a date strip, receipt, map scrap, or paper label;
- one piece of semi-transparent tape or one stamped mark, not both repeatedly;
- one brass or wet-rose accent.

Torn edges are decorative boundaries, never touch targets. Text and buttons sit on clean rectangular interior areas. Photo fragments may overlap by 8–16px while key subjects remain unobstructed.

### Screen rhythm

- Alternate dark walnut environments with oat-paper reading surfaces.
- Use thin brass or rain-glass rules instead of heavy borders.
- Depth comes from real material logic: paper sits above wood; tracing paper sits above photography; moisture reflections sit behind UI.
- Use low, broad paper shadows only on collage fragments. Standard controls use no shadow.
- Keep 20px mobile gutters and a 4/8px spacing system.

### Module differentiation

- Record: contact-sheet or diary-spread collage, calendar as a torn ledger page.
- Create: darkroom/editing-table mood; photo remains largest; free tools remain primary; exactly two AI recommendations appear as paper swatches.
- Publish: poster assembly board with a clean 4:5 canvas and movable-looking but stable fragments.
- Mine: private passport/field-notebook page with stamps, stats, membership and privacy on readable paper panels.

### Motion

- Short paper-settle motion on save, 180–240ms.
- Slow rain-glass shimmer only for AI queued/running states; reduced motion removes shimmer.
- No floating particles, page-long parallax, or constant paper wobble.

### Anti-patterns

- No generic scrapbook explosion, washi-tape everywhere, fake handwritten body copy, sepia over every photo, bright café-chain green, neon blue, heavy black luxury UI, or illegible low-contrast brown-on-brown.
- Do not turn all cards into torn shapes. Limit the effect so it remains special.
- Do not let atmosphere hide task state, price, credit use, or failure/refund information.
