## 最终提示词

Use case: ui-mockup.

Create one original, high-fidelity mobile product design review board for a Chinese drink-life journal app. Wide 16:9 landscape presentation, warm ivory paper background, four complete modern smartphone frames side by side, no Apple logo and no copied brand identity. This is a polished Figma-like UI concept, not an illustration of phones and not a website screenshot.

Overall visual identity: “诗性纸刊 / Poetic Paper Journal” for artsy young women who love milk tea, coffee, photography, journaling, exhibitions and quiet city life. Understated Chinese editorial design: warm rice-paper #F6F0E6, ivory cards #FFFCF6, cocoa-black ink #302A27, tea brown #986143, dusty rose #C6928B, quiet moss #737D6A, one tiny persimmon accent #B75B46. Fine editorial rules, generous negative space, restrained paper grain, realistic natural-light drink photography, elegant Chinese serif display type paired with clean sans-serif UI type. Moderate 12–18px corners, almost no shadows. Clear, useful, accessible product UI. Do not make it cute, childish, overly pink, luxury-black, neon, glassmorphic, glossy, cluttered scrapbook, or an endless waterfall feed. No emojis as icons. Use one consistent delicate line-icon family.

Show four different core modules, each visibly distinct but from one system:

PHONE 1 — 记录. Large readable Chinese header “九月，慢慢喝”, small editorial label “ISSUE 09 · SHANGHAI”. One hero 4:5 photograph of jasmine milk tea on a sunlit wooden café table with soft plant shadow. Primary action “记一杯”, secondary action “从相册选”. Below it, a compact September calendar with a few dates containing tiny drink-photo thumbnails and a two-column recent-memory preview. Calm, immediate, not feed-like.

PHONE 2 — 创作. Large readable header “创作”. Big drink-photo canvas with before/after control. Bottom tool row with “滤镜  调整  裁切  版式  AI”. A free-filter thumbnail rail. Below/near it a clear section reading “AI 为你选了 2 种”, with EXACTLY TWO recommendation cards only: “纸上留影” and “丝网记忆”. Each card has a tiny original preview, one short reason, and a subtle member mark. Make free editing primary and AI membership secondary, transparent, never aggressive.

PHONE 3 — 发布. Large readable header “发布”. Segmented tabs “小红书” and “朋友圈”, with 小红书 selected. Show a real 4:5 editorial post preview combining the drink photo, generous cream margin, small date and one poetic line. Beneath or beside it show three editable sections: “标题”, “正文”, “标签”. Fixed actions “保存图片”, “复制文案”, “系统分享”. No claim that it has already posted.

PHONE 4 — 我的. Large readable header “我的”. A quiet paper passport profile card with female avatar placeholder, name “林间小满”, and private-status indicator. Monthly summary with “本月 12 杯”, taste note, a small row of original city/drink passport stamps, “我的月刊”, and a transparent membership card reading “本月剩余 8 次”. Include “隐私与数据” entry. No fabricated partner brands.

All four phones have a consistent bottom navigation with exactly four labels and line icons: “记录  创作  发布  我的”, and the correct current tab selected on each phone. Add only a small board title outside the phones: “诗性纸刊 · V1 视觉方向”. Make all large Chinese labels legible and correctly spelled; avoid tiny filler text and avoid gibberish. Keep the board presentation clean, premium, original and suitable for a serious design review.
